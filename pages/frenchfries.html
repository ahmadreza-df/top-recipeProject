Classic, Crispy French Fries Recipe

Home Page

Golden, crispy on the outside, and perfectly fluffy on the inside. these homemade french fries are the ultimate side dish and way better than frozen store-bought ones.

Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes (Russet or similar)
  • Vegetable oil (for deep frying)
  • 1 teaspoon salt (or to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika or garlic powder (optional, for extra flavor)
  • Cold water (for soaking)

Instructions

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes, then cut them into thin, even strips (about 1/4 inch thick)
  2. Place the cut potatoes in a large bowl of cold water and let them soak for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch.
  3. Drain the water and pat the potatoes completely dry with a clean kitchen towel (this is crucial for crispy fries!)
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pot or fryer to 160°C (325°F). Fry the potatoes in small batches for 3-4 minutes until soft but not browned. Remove and let them rest.
  5. Increase the oil temperature to 190°C (375°F). Fry the potatoes a second time for 2-3 minutes until they are golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove from the oil, drain on paper towels, and immediately sprinkle with salt and paprika.
